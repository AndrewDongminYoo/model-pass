# 2026 Makeup Exam Model-Condition Source Check

Accessed on 2026-09-24: [Q-net's 2026 makeup practical-exam notice](https://www.q-net.or.kr/cst006.do?artlSeq=5250969&brdId=Q006&gSite=Q&id=cst00602), published on 2025-12-30.
The notice provides a ZIP attachment titled `2026년 미용사(메이크업) 큐넷 공지용.zip` (download SHA-256: `ca2be34ff3e426fd5e0f6d50c1959cd352e2c9a08dd48b42ee1445598f1ee0c5`).
The checks below use its 2026 public task document and FAQ, not the older 2021 template URL.

## Verified Source Statements

- The public task document's common instructions set the model age at 14–55 by calendar year, require the model to arrive without makeup and with an accepted identity document, and prohibit visible identifying accessories or nail markings.
- The same document lists permanent or semi-permanent eyebrow, eyeliner, or lip makeup, eyelash extensions, eyebrow dye, and tint as separate score-deduction cases for the examinee; it does not describe those cases as an absolute bar on bringing the model.
- FAQ question 10 says a model with tattoos, henna, or bleached hair may take part without a separate score deduction.
- FAQ question 36 confirms that an eyebrow-tattoo, semi-permanent-makeup, eyebrow-dye, or tint model may be brought but incurs a deduction.
- FAQ question 8 prohibits circle or colored contact lenses and identifying accessories or nail markings for the model.
- An examinee/model same-sex requirement was not verified in the reviewed 2026 public task document or FAQ. This is a source gap, not proof that no venue-specific requirement exists.

## Product Decision

On 2026-09-24, the operator chose to align version 2 with the reviewed official source: omit tattoos and henna as exclusions, treat score-deduction cases as recruiter review items, and label model sex as a recruiter-specified condition rather than an official same-sex restriction.
Version 1 snapshots remain readable but are not promoted as current exam rules.
The platform's adult-only boundary remains 19+, regardless of the exam's 14–55 model range.
Version 2 retains the official upper bound of 55 by the 2026 exam's birth-year rule; submission derives that bound from the applicant's date of birth rather than trusting a yes/no answer.
The applicant enters the birth date during the condition check; the client and submission server use the same 19+ Seoul-birthday calculation and 2026 exam birth-year upper-bound calculation.
The 2026 template cannot publish a makeup appointment outside the 2026 Seoul exam year; a later exam year requires a newly sourced version.
